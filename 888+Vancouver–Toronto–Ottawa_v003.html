One-time tour timing basis: crossing records alone define the tour's stops. Each of the 888 listed grade-crossing records is treated as one tour stop with a five-minute halt, including the first and last records. The moving-time estimate uses the schematic connector distance between consecutive geocoded crossing records in this map, not surveyed railway mileage; records without coordinates still receive their five-minute halt but do not add mapped distance. Scope, source and review notes

VIA Rail’s route page identifies The Canadian as its Toronto–Vancouver cross-country train. The appended Toronto–Ottawa records are an additional inventory route extension and are not represented as part of The Canadian itself. The section labels and crossing assignment come from the supplied review workbook. The original 572 crossing IDs were checked against the supplied Crosings Data.csv. A further 316 Toronto–Ottawa records were appended from the verified extraction; their TC IDs do not duplicate any of the original 572 IDs.

  • The original 572 records have active FLB or FLBG protection. The added Toronto–Ottawa set contains 209 active FLB/FLBG records plus 102 Passive and 5 Other records, as supplied. Public and private access records are retained.
  • Seventeen records in the merged 888-record set have no usable latitude/longitude and cannot be plotted; all remain in the detail table. Four are from the original set and 13 are from the added Toronto–Ottawa set.
  • Three records in one location group share the same coordinate: Cascade ID 20578 (Chaumox Rd) and Thompson IDs 29246 (Hwy 12) and 29239 (Hwy 8). The Thompson entries are flagged in the table and map for coordinate review.
  • This is an inventory-based list, not a field survey. Transport Canada notes that the inventory is updated periodically and may not contain every current field detail. Confirm flagged locations and warning-system status before operational use.

Sources: VIA Rail — The Canadian route; Transport Canada — Grade Crossings Inventory; Transport Canada — FLB and FLB & G definitions. The supplied review workbook labels its inventory extract “2023 update; data through 2024-10-09.”
