Scope, source and review notes

VIA Rail’s route page identifies The Canadian as its Toronto–Vancouver cross-country train. The section labels and crossing assignment come from the supplied review workbook. The 572 crossing IDs were checked against the supplied Crosings Data.csv; all IDs were present, unique and had matching protection, milepost, landmark and coordinate fields.

  • All 572 records have an active FLB or FLBG code in the supplied inventory data. The set includes private-access crossings as well as public ones.
  • Four inventory records have no latitude/longitude and cannot be plotted (TC IDs 777955, 779522, 52268 and 777938); all remain in the detail table.
  • Three records in one location group share the same coordinate: Cascade ID 20578 (Chaumox Rd) and Thompson IDs 29246 (Hwy 12) and 29239 (Hwy 8). The Thompson entries are flagged in the table and map for coordinate review.
  • This is an inventory-based list, not a field survey. Transport Canada notes that the inventory is updated periodically and may not contain every current field detail. Confirm flagged locations and warning-system status before operational use.
